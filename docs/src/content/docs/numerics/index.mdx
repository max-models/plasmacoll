plasmacoll collides charged markers with prescribed neutral gases by the null-collision Monte
Carlo method: the collision step of a particle-in-cell code, or of a homogeneous reactor in a
constant electric field. Around the operator it has the cross sections (tables and fits), their
rate coefficients over a Maxwellian or any electron distribution, a two-term Boltzmann solver
for the electrons of a gas mixture and the charge-state fractions of a fast beam, all from the
same `CollisionProcess` objects. Collisions never change the targets: the gas is an input.

Each equation on these pages is declared on the code that implements it, as a method card, and
checked by the tests: 35 closed forms and fits against SymPy rules, seven discretisations against
their order of convergence. The pages of the section list the cards module by module; this one
shows how they fit together.

## The collision step

Markers are positions, velocities and weights in NumPy or CuPy arrays; a product takes the
position and weight of the incident marker.

1. **[Neutral backgrounds](/plasmacoll/numerics/background/):** drifting Maxwellians with
   density and temperature profiles, from which each candidate draws a partner velocity.
2. **[Cross sections](/plasmacoll/numerics/cross-sections/)** tabulated against the
   centre-of-mass or the lab energy, linear between their points.
3. **[Null-collision method](/plasmacoll/numerics/mcc/):** the frequency of every process, its
   bound over all speeds, and the step: a marker is a candidate with probability
   $1 - e^{-\nu_{\max}\Delta t}$ and then collides with process $k$ with probability
   $\nu_k/\nu_{\max}$:

<MethodCard name="plasmacoll.mcc.MonteCarloCollisions.collide" headingLevel={3} />

4. **[Collision kinematics](/plasmacoll/numerics/process/)** of each kind of process
   (elastic, excitation, ionization with its energy sharing, attachment, charge exchange,
   stripping, beam ionization...) in the centre-of-mass frame, with the scattering angles of
   the null-collision page.
5. **[Merging and splitting](/plasmacoll/numerics/population/)** keep the number of markers in
   bounds, conserving weight, momentum and energy; the
   **[zero-dimensional reactor](/plasmacoll/numerics/reactor/)** adds a field and repeats the
   step.

## Swarms, rates and beams

- **[Rate coefficients](/plasmacoll/numerics/rates/):** $\langle\sigma g\rangle$ over a
  Maxwellian of relative speeds, tabulated in log-log, and the rate over a tabulated electron
  energy distribution.
- **[Two-term Boltzmann solver](/plasmacoll/numerics/boltzmann/):** the steady electron energy
  distribution in a reduced field, with Scharfetter-Gummel fluxes and collision integrals that
  conserve the particles exactly, and from it the transport and rate coefficients:

<MethodCard name="plasmacoll.boltzmann.solve_eedf" headingLevel={3} />

- **[Charge states](/plasmacoll/numerics/charge-states/)** of a fast beam along a path, by the
  matrix exponential of the transition matrices, and the same cross sections as Monte Carlo
  processes; **[beam in gas](/plasmacoll/numerics/beam-gas/)** assembles them for a hydrogen or
  deuterium beam in H₂.

## Data

The fits are cards too, with their sources and ranges of validity:
[H₂ and H⁻ (Janev, Reiter and Samm)](/plasmacoll/numerics/janev/),
[hydrogen atoms](/plasmacoll/numerics/atomic-hydrogen/),
[elastic e + H, H₂ (Park et al.)](/plasmacoll/numerics/hydrogen-elastic/),
[secondary electrons of fast ions (Rudd)](/plasmacoll/numerics/secondaries/),
[Ar⁺ in argon (Phelps)](/plasmacoll/numerics/argon/),
[metals (Lotz)](/plasmacoll/numerics/metals/), [caesium](/plasmacoll/numerics/caesium/) and
[vibrational populations](/plasmacoll/numerics/vibrational/). Electron-argon sets are LXCat
files the user downloads; the proton tables of `proton_hydrogen_processes` are inputs.

## Units and conventions

Energies and temperatures ($kT$) in eV, masses, speeds, densities and cross sections in SI
(CODATA 2018; $e$ and $k_B$ exact), the reduced field in Td ($10^{-21}$ V m²). Electron cross
sections are looked up at the electron's energy with the target at rest (lab frame), ion-neutral
ones mostly at the centre-of-mass energy; every process says which. $F_0$ is normalised by
$\int \varepsilon^{1/2} F_0\,d\varepsilon = 1$.

## Not in the model

- Coulomb collisions, recombination, and collisions between two tracked species (except a
  tracked species as the target of a `DynamicProfile`, which the collisions do not change).
- More than one collision per marker and step; products collide from the next step on.
- The energy and momentum of untracked partners: detachment fragments, the ion recoil in
  electron-impact ionization, the recoils in beam ionization, the binding energies in stripping.
- Gas depletion and heating: the consumed neutrals are reported, nothing is solved for the gas.
- In the Boltzmann solver: superelastic collisions, the gas temperature in the inelastic terms,
  multi-term corrections, time dependence, electron-electron collisions.
- Vibrational kinetics: the populations are a prescribed Boltzmann distribution.
- Data: the beam-gas placeholders, deuterium from hydrogen at equal speed, the capture fit above
  200 keV, Park's momentum transfer above a few hundred eV, Lotz without the deeper subshells.

## Verification

Besides the checks of the cards, the tests compare the collided fraction with
$(1 - e^{-\nu_{\max}\Delta t})\nu/\nu_{\max}$, the relaxation of ions and electrons with closed
forms of Maxwell molecules (temperature, drift velocity, Wannier's energy), the Boltzmann solver
with the Maxwellian and Druyvesteyn distributions, Reid's and the Lucas-Saelee model gases and
BOLOS, the charge-state fractions and the beam markers with each other, and the fits with the
values printed in their sources.
