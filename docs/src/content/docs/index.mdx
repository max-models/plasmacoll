## What it does

<CardGrid>
	<Card title="Null-collision MCC" icon="random">
		The method of Vahedi and Surendra (1995). Elastic and backscatter collisions, excitation,
		ionization, attachment, detachment and charge transfer against Maxwellian neutral gases,
		uniform or with a density profile.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Plain arrays, CPU or GPU" icon="laptop">
		Markers are positions, velocities and weights in NumPy or CuPy arrays through
		[cunumpy](https://pypi.org/project/cunumpy/). Any particle container that has them plugs in.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Cross sections" icon="document">
		Tables from text and LXCat files or from functions, Maxwell-molecule cross sections for
		benchmarks, and population-weighted mixtures of level-resolved cross sections.
	</Card>
	<Card title="0D reactor" icon="setting">
		A homogeneous reactor advanced by collisions alone, for testing cross-section sets and
		reaction chains before a spatial simulation.
	</Card>
</CardGrid>
